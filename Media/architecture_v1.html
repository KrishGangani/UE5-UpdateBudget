UpdateBudget

A frame-time budget for gameplay logic. Every agent gets a deadline from its significance. Each frame the scheduler runs the agents that would otherwise miss their deadline, then the rest in deadline order, until the next update would not fit in the budget.

Rules that keep it safe

Game and PIE worlds only, registered at begin-playA custom tick starts the moment it is registered, and world subsystems also exist in editor worlds.
Unregister in EndPlay, guard with IsValidGarbage collection nulls a destroyed component only when it runs; until then the pointer is stale.
Defer adds and removes during the loopAn update can destroy its own actor or spawn a new one synchronously.
Staleness in seconds, not framesA 10-frame cap is 333 ms at 30 fps and 69 ms at 144 fps; a gameplay guarantee must not depend on frame rate.
Equal measurement cost in every modeEvery update records its gap the same way, so the baselines carry no extra instrumentation.
Credit the prior artCost smoothing and accumulated delta come from Epic's Animation Budget Allocator, adapted here to general gameplay logic.
